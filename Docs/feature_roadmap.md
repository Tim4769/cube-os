# Cube OS Feature Roadmap


- The Telepresence Network enables Wi-Fi communication between robots via an online server.
- Two 9 g servos power a four-bar walking linkage for bipedal locomotion.
- IMU-assisted stepping, turning, fall detection, and recovery are examples of inertial gait control.
- Light-aware quiet mode for sleep, privacy, and do-not-disturb behavior is called Ambient Rest Mode.
- Desk climate sensing measures humidity and temperature to provide local air data.
- Voice Mailbox: Use the integrated microphone and speaker to record and replay voice notes from friends.
- USB-C Power Workflow: battery-powered operation, serial debugging, charging, and flashing.
- Camera sharing: sending OV2640 pictures or brief videos to friends or the dashboard.
- Widgets, expressions, friend feeds, time, status, and air data are all included in the Four-Face Display OS.
