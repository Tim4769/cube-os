# Main body design and component mount brainstorm

I first wanted to design the main body of the robot with the newly designed motor holder

- To keep the main body a cube with 80 * 80 * 80mm dimensions → I found that I will only have a height of around 55 mm to play with to host all the components as the motor will eat up the rest of the 25 mm

![Screenshot 2026-05-14 at 21.43.58.png](/user-attachments/blobs/redirect/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTU2MzcsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--0950dc212f6d5636a4fe6de8d6823d0c96a20035/Screenshot 2026-05-14 at 21.43.58.png)

I implemented the design I thought about before with the back panel being removable for assembly and any maintenance or fixes that needs to be carried out on the robot.

I then brainstormed the methods to mount the screens as it does not come with mounting holes → I landed with mounting the screen from the inside to avoid any wires being exposed

- It will be mounted right up to the inside wall of the main body with a carved in slot so only the active area will be exposed
- It will then be fixed using a back panel which is essentially a flat plate with extrusion on the sides to allow screwing directly into the inside wall of the body with threads for strength

We also found out that the original display panel that we found were only 3 * 3 cm which would look extremely small on the 8 * 8 side of the cube so we are planning to find a 5 * 5 cm screen to make it look more proportional

- The 5 * 5 size will also allow the mounting of the sourced camera on the side of the screen so the robot can have a visual feedback mechanism

The thickness of the walls of the main body is 4 mm as we want to keep it as thin as possible to keep as much space inside as possible while also allowing the M3 screws to be used to fix the back panel to the main body.
