# Designing Motor holder

Started designing holder for the TS90A 9g micro servo

- I found the f3d file for the motor on the fusion community

![Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.19.18.png](/user-attachments/blobs/redirect/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTUyOTIsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--3f5e6e78567e0d6df389b9c2413d29faaf861e1d/Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.19.18.png)

I edited the motor attachment part so that it can fit nicely with the motor mounter:

![Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.19.39.png](/user-attachments/blobs/redirect/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTUyOTMsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--59c70fa89608ddcf77ee00e8e4ea7f26cd4efdbb/Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.19.39.png)

Then created the mount for the motor which would be the under side of the CubeOS main body

- I designed it so that it will be slid in from the side of the cube for easier assembly and disassembly and will be mounted through the mounting hole directly onto the main body with threads

![Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.19.58.png](/user-attachments/blobs/redirect/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTUyOTQsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--827588bd81c2d6b78840680ee8df130616dfcec0/Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.19.58.png)
![Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.20.14.png](/user-attachments/blobs/redirect/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTUyOTUsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--e99f946e3df1a00a54040ce862d10660372a6580/Screenshot 2026-05-13 at 22.20.14.png)

The hole I created is to create enough space for the motor to actually spin without getting stuck while also allowing space for a screw driver to access the mounting holes for assembly. I then used assembly features in fusion 360 to properly assemble the motor with the motor attachment and linkages to simulate everything moving and there seems to be no physical clash.

Next Steps:

- Create a wiring hole for the motor
- The motor holder cannot be tested physically because it will arrive later.
