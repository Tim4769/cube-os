Removed MLX90632SLD-DCB-000-RE
Removed SCD41-D-R2

The first is a CO2 sensor, and the second is a long-wavelength sensor. They could provide additional features like an air quality tracker that sits on your desk, but due to the expensive components and the primary function of connecting friends from long distances, these sensors are omitted to ensure affordability.

After removing these components, the PCB cost with production cost is 144 dollars per 2 boards with SMT assembly, so around 70 dollars per robot.
