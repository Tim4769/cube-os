The mechanical team and I decided to use the [2.4-inch screen](https://ronboelectronics.en.made-in-china.com/product/pFOaqvkHkWci/China-2-4-Inch-Resolution-Qvga-240X320-12pin-Spi-FPC-Zif-Connector-Type-IPS-LCD-Displays.html) for our new screen since it is much bigger than the previous one

So I changed the symbol and footprint in Kicad to suit the screen, but while I was retracing the connections, after tracing 3 out of 4 connectors, I realized I had to rotate connector 180° so that the opening side faces outward, which is the opposite of the last connector. 

When tracing, I also realized that we will probably need an extender for the screen cables, since the default cables that come with the screen are pretty short, and the PCB won’t be in the center anyway. It looks something like this:
