# Fabrication Notes

## Board

- Layers: 4-layer FR4
- Thickness: 1.599 mm in the KiCad stackup
- Finish: HAL lead-free in the KiCad stackup
- Copper: 35 um outer layers, 17 um inner layers in the KiCad stackup
- Board outline: approximately 60 mm x 60 mm, with a left-side edge cutout
- Solder mask: manufacturer default unless a final color is specified before ordering
- Silkscreen: manufacturer default unless a final color is specified before ordering

## Assembly

- Assembly side is top-side SMT plus top-side connectors.
- Confirm whether the assembly house will place the USB-C connector, JST connectors, servo pin headers, FPC connectors, microphone, and switches, or whether some parts should be hand-soldered after PCBA.
- Verify display/camera FPC connector orientation before ordering.
- Verify servo header orientation against the enclosure and linkage CAD before soldering.
- Mounting holes H1-H4 are mechanical features, not BOM placement items.
