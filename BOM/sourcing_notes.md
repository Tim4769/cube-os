# Sourcing Notes

- PCB: current team estimate is 144 USD for the minimum production quantity of 2 boards. Confirm whether this includes assembly, components, shipping, taxes, and tooling before final budget submission.
- Servos: [MakerTech Store 9g micro servo motors](https://www.makertechstore.com/products/9g-micro-servo-motor), 2 required.
- Camera: [Taidacent OV2640 module](https://www.amazon.com/Taidacent-Million-Wide-Angle-Interface-Standard/dp/B08XJZZ287/ref=sr_1_1?sr=8-1), 1 required.
- Speaker: [PUI Audio AS02504AR](https://www.digikey.ch/en/products/detail/pui-audio-inc/AS02504AR/24385606), 1 required.

- Displays: [ER-TFT1.54-2 display panels](https://www.ebay.com/itm/306891693859), 4 required.

## Alternatives

- Servo alternatives must fit the mechanical linkage torque, horn, screw, and package constraints. Verify stall current before connecting to the Rev A servo rail.
- Camera alternatives must match the OV2640 FPC pinout or require a PCB/adapter change.
- Speaker alternatives should match the amplifier load, enclosure volume, grille design, and connector.
- Battery alternatives must match 1S LiPo chemistry, protection requirements, JST connector/polarity, enclosure size, and charger current.
- Display alternatives must match the FPC pinout, voltage, controller, active area, and mounting orientation.

- Order extra servos, displays, FPC cables/modules, and fasteners if the budget allows; these are likely to fail or wear during gait testing.
